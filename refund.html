Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DD Month YYYY]

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for payments made to Arun Enterprises through the Driver Mitra app and this website.

Daily rent & scheme payments

Payments made toward daily rent and dues are for services already provided or due, and are generally non-refundable once successfully processed, except where a payment was charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge).

Onboarding (₹50,000) & the Drive to Own agreement

Refund eligibility for the ₹50,000 onboarding amount and any other scheme payments is governed by the written agreement you sign at our office. Please review that agreement before making a payment; it sets out the conditions, if any, under which amounts may be refunded or adjusted.

Payment charged in error

If you believe you were charged incorrectly or twice, contact us within [X days, e.g. 7 days] of the transaction with the payment reference. Verified erroneous charges are refunded to the original payment method, typically within [5–7] business days after verification. Refund timelines to your bank are determined by Razorpay and your bank.

How to request

Raise a request through your fleet office or write to [contact email] with your name, mobile number, and the payment reference. See Contact Us.

Because the Drive to Own scheme involves a signed agreement, the specific refund terms for scheme payments are those in your agreement. Speak to our team at the office for anything not covered here.
